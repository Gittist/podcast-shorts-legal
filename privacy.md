This Privacy Policy explains how the application podcast-shorts (the App) handles data. The App is a private tool used to upload short videos to the operator's own social media accounts, including YouTube and TikTok.

	1.	Who we are. The App is operated by an individual developer for personal use. Contact: paul.wirfs@icloud.com
	2.	What data we access. When you authorize the App with your TikTok account, the App receives an access token that allows it to upload videos to the connected account and manage the videos it has uploaded. The App only accesses the account of the operator who installed and authorized it. It does not access, collect, or process data from any other TikTok users.
	3.	How we use the data. The access token is used solely to publish and manage the operator's own short videos. The App does not use the data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than uploading content.
	4.	Data storage. The access token is stored locally on the operator's own device, in the App's configuration. It is not transmitted to any third party other than TikTok's official API for the purpose of uploading videos.
	5.	Data sharing. We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Data is only exchanged directly with the official APIs of the platforms the operator connects, such as TikTok and YouTube.
	6.	Data retention and deletion. The access token is kept only as long as the App is in use. The operator can revoke access at any time by removing the App from the account's connected apps settings, and by deleting the local configuration file.
	7.	Changes to this policy. This policy may be updated. The current version is always available at this URL.
	8.	Contact. For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: paul.wirfs@icloud.com
